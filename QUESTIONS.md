# Questions

When you can't tell *why* something is the way it is, or SPEC.md is ambiguous, don't guess.
Append a block below, skip the task, and work on the next one. The harness sends new
questions to Gio; answers appear in ANSWERS.md.

Format (one block per question):

    ---
    task: <task id>
    question: <one or two sentences>
    checked: <what you looked at before asking>
